About Me

Maker, Developer,
STEM educator.

I build things, break them, learn from both. Based in Kozhikode — curious about everything.

Hi — I'm Muhammed Basheer, a maker and developer from Kozhikode, Kerala. I work at the intersection of hardware and software, building systems that live in the real world: IoT devices, robots, tools, and interactive installations.

My background spans embedded systems, web development, and physical computing. If I can prototype it on a breadboard or ship it as code, I'm genuinely happy. The process of turning an idea into a real, working thing is what keeps me going.

"The best way to understand something is to build it wrong first."

Alongside building, I work as a STEM Consultant — designing workshops and curricula that help students engage with electronics and programming hands-on. Getting someone excited about making something for the first time is genuinely one of my favourite things.

I share what I build on Instructables and GitHub. When I'm not at a workbench or a keyboard, I'm probably reading about something I don't yet understand.
